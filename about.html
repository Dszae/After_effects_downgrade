About the Project

As a video editor and computer engineering student, I frequently encountered Adobe After Effects’ restrictive upgrade blocking error: “This file was created with a newer version of After Effects — time to upgrade your application!”

Instead of forcing creative artists and production studios into costly upgrades or complicated scripts, I reverse-engineered the big-endian RIFX container binary format. After Effects Downgrader directly patches the 20-byte head chunk in .aep project files and the 16-byte parameter stream schema in .ffx animation presets.

The application operates 100% locally in your web browser using JavaScript TypedArrays and WebAssembly. No files, project metadata, or assets ever touch a remote server, ensuring total confidentiality for commercial productions.

Frequently asked questions

How do I downgrade an After Effects (.aep) project?

Drop your .aep or .aet file into the converter, choose your target release (such as After Effects 2024 or 2023), and click Downgrade. The tool rewrites the RIFX container version byte and XMP metadata so your project opens without version upgrade prompts.

How to fix "This file was created with a newer version of After Effects"?

This dialog occurs when After Effects detects a newer major build byte in the file's binary header chunk. This utility modifies that internal signature, allowing earlier versions to parse and open the composition structure.

Why does a downgraded .ffx preset apply nothing in After Effects?

In After Effects, animation presets require an active layer (such as an Adjustment Layer or Solid) selected in your timeline before applying. Additionally, if the preset relies on third-party plugins (like Boris FX Sapphire or Deep Glow), those plugins must be installed on your workstation.

Are project files uploaded to any server?

No. Conversion is performed entirely within your browser using JavaScript TypedArrays and WebAssembly. Your files, composition data, and assets never leave your local machine.
